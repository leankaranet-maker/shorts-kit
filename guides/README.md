## The seven things that caused the most revisions before

1. **Retakes left in.** Alignment can stretch a word across a pause or merge a restart into one sentence. Read every «stretched» line from `build` (guide 02).
2. **Pauses.** No pause over 0.3 s anywhere. `qa` checks it twice (word gaps and the audio itself).
3. **Captions on the face or under the app buttons.** Captions sit under the chin, centred, never below y 1404.
4. **The camera panning.** One fixed frame per Short; shots only zoom in and out.
5. **The hook card repeating the spoken words.** The hook is the viewer's pain as a short question, in other words.
6. **A CTA nobody said.** No CTA unless the clip says one.
7. **Wrong words on screen.** Whisper's spelling mistakes are fixed per word (`fix`) or for the channel (`channel.json` `fixes`). Check every caption page in the stills.

If they ask for something these guides contradict, they win. Then add the rule
to `06-house-rules.md` in the same session so the next batch starts from it.
